# Privacy

A11yCompass analyzes only the webpage you choose to review.

Page content and accessibility findings are processed locally in your browser and are not sent to a remote server.

The extension asks Chrome for access to http(s) pages so it can inspect the tab you choose to review. That access is used only to run the review in your browser. Page content is not uploaded.

Reports remain on your device unless you explicitly export or copy them.

Settings, rule preferences, and local manual-review progress may be stored with `chrome.storage` on this device.
