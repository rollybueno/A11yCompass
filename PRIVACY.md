# Privacy

A11yCompass analyzes only the webpage you choose to review.

Page content and accessibility findings are processed locally in your browser and are not sent to a remote server.

The extension does not receive access to websites when it is installed. Chrome will ask for access to the current http(s) page when you open the review panel or start a review. That access is used only to inspect the page in your browser. Page content is not uploaded.

Local files (`file://`) are not reviewed.

Reports remain on your device unless you explicitly export or copy them.

Settings, rule preferences, and local manual-review progress may be stored with `chrome.storage` on this device.

The extension does not sell user data, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with third parties.
